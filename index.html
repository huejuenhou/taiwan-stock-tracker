Daily gain / loss

Each day is the change in closing price × the shares held. On days you buy or sell, it uses your trade price instead, and that trade's fees and tax count on that day. Adding up every day gives Total return on the Portfolio page. Dividends aren't included, so an ETF's price drop on its ex-dividend date shows as a loss here.

Expected payments

Estimates follow each fund's recent payouts and dates until the fund announces the real ones. Shares are what each person holds the day before the ex-dividend date. Use Edit to type the correct date and amount. Dividends stay on this page and are not part of the Portfolio totals.
